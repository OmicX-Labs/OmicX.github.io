What we offer
Our core services
End-to-end omics data analysis — from raw sequencing reads to publication-ready biological insights.
01
Transcriptomics & RNA-seq
Bulk and single-sample RNA-seq analysis — differential expression, transcript quantification, splicing analysis, gene fusion detection, and functional enrichment across any model organism or tissue.
STAR · HISAT2 · DESeq2 · edgeR · salmon
02
Clinical & Variant Genomics
WES/WGS variant calling pipelines for clinical applications — SNP/indel/CNV detection, ACMG-based classification, pharmacogenomics, and report generation for inherited disease and oncology panels.
GATK4 · BWA-MEM · VEP · Franklin · ClinVar
03
Metagenomics
Amplicon (16S/ITS) and shotgun metagenomics — taxonomic profiling, diversity analysis (alpha/beta), functional annotation, and differential abundance across treatment groups.
QIIME2 · DADA2 · Kraken2 · HUMAnN3 · phyloseq
04
Single-cell analysis
snRNA-seq, snATAC-seq, and 10x Multiome processing — cell clustering, trajectory inference, peak calling, TF motif enrichment, and WNN multimodal integration.
Seurat · Signac · ArchR · Harmony · MACS2
05
Proteomics
Label-free and TMT-based quantitative proteomics — peptide identification, protein-level differential abundance, PTM analysis, and pathway enrichment from LC-MS/MS data.
MaxQuant · Perseus · MSstats · STRING · R
06
Statistical analysis
Rigorous biostatistical consulting — experimental design, power analysis, linear/mixed models, survival analysis, and publication-quality visualization for any biological dataset.
R · Python · GraphPad · ggplot2 · ComplexHeatmap
07
Multiomics data analysis
Joint analysis of multiple omics layers — integrating transcriptomics, epigenomics, and proteomics to reveal regulatory networks and identify cross-layer biomarkers.
MOFA+ · mixOmics · DIABLO · WNN · R
08
Integrative multiomics
Deep multi-layer integration combining scRNA-seq, scATAC-seq, spatial transcriptomics, and clinical metadata — linking chromatin accessibility to gene expression and phenotype.
Seurat · Signac · SCENIC · ArchR · 10x Multiome
09
Pipeline development & automation
Custom reproducible, containerized bioinformatics pipelines — scalable from laptop to HPC/cloud, with full documentation and version control for any omics workflow.
Snakemake · Nextflow · Docker · Conda · Git

About OmicX Labs
Fast, flexible & highly skilled

OmicX Labs is a freelance bioinformatics team based in Cairo, Egypt. We provide end-to-end data analysis solutions across genomics, transcriptomics, metagenomics, RNA-seq, NGS workflows, primer design, machine learning, and AI-driven pipelines.

Our workflow is built for speed, accuracy, and clarity. Every project is reviewed by specialized consultants to ensure high-quality, publication-ready results for researchers, academics, clinicians, and pharmaceutical teams.
